Seasonal Trends

Each species' share of that week's total detections — not raw counts — over a rolling 26-week window. Dividing by the week's total cancels out anything that shifts detection volume for every species equally, like a mic change or a stretch of bad weather, so what's left is closer to real seasonal activity.
