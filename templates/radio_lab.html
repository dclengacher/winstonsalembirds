The setup

Home: Raspberry Pi 4 running BirdNET-Pi and the WittBoy weather station, with an H4 Slim Pro GPS node (Meshtastic) and a BTECH UV-Pro + Digirig Mobile for APRS. Mesh nodes: SenseCAP T1000 and 2× RAK WisMesh Pocket v3, spread across about half a mile. APRS (next phase): Yaesu FT65 about 3 miles away, so the two radio systems can be compared on the same events.
